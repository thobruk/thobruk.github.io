Most Learning Data Stops at Activity

Traditional learning systems can track:

  • completions
  • attendance
  • survey responses
  • training participation

But they rarely provide visibility into:

  • what skills employees developed
  • whether those skills are applied on the job
  • how learning impacts workforce capability over time

Without measurable skill data, organizations struggle to understand workforce capability and readiness.
