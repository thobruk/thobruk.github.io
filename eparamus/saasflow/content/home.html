The Shift

The Problem Isn't Measurement.
It's Precise Design.

Most learning programs are built around content delivery—not clearly defined skill outcomes. Without a structured way to define what people should be able to do, under what conditions, and to what level of performance, organizations struggle to measure whether learning is truly effective.

When learning is designed around measurable, job-relevant skills, organizations can finally verify what was learned, what is being applied, and where capability gaps still exist.

Clear skill definition is what makes meaningful measurement possible.

The System

A System for Building Measurable Capability

IMPACT helps organizations define, build, and verify workforce capability through a structured, measurable learning process. By connecting learning design, skill verification, and workforce insight, organizations gain clear visibility into what learning is actually producing.
