The Process

Why Measurable Design Changes Everything

Most learning programs are designed primarily to organize and deliver information. But information alone does not guarantee workforce capability.

To verify whether learning is effective, organizations must first define:

  • The skills employees need to perform
  • The conditions in which performance occurs
  • The criteria that determine successful application on the job

When learning is designed around measurable performance expectations, organizations gain the ability to align learning to organizational needs, verify skill development, measure application on the job, and continuously improve learning effectiveness over time.

Measurable design is what makes workforce capability visibility possible.
