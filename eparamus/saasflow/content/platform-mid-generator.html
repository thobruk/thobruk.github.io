The Challenge

Most Learning Design Is Built Manually—and Inconsistently

Instructional designers are often expected to build effective learning programs quickly using disconnected content, inconsistent SME input, and limited time. As a result: learning quality varies, measurable outcomes are often unclear, and skill verification becomes difficult to achieve consistently.

Without a structured design process, organizations struggle to standardize learning effectiveness and workforce capability measurement.

The Solution

A Structured Assistant for Measurable Learning Design

The MID Generator uses measurable instructional design principles to automatically generate structured learning blueprints aligned to job-relevant skills and performance expectations. Instead of starting from scratch, instructional designers begin with a strong measurable design foundation that can be reviewed, refined, and customized.

The Difference

Designed Around Skills—Not Just Content

Unlike traditional course development approaches that focus primarily on organizing information, the MID Generator structures learning around measurable job-relevant skills. This includes: defining expected performance, identifying conditions for success, aligning evaluations to skill outcomes, and supporting verification of learning application on the job.

The result is learning designed to consistently build measurable capability—not just deliver content.
