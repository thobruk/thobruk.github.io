**🟩 SECTION 2 --- THE CHALLENGE**

**Headline**

**Most Learning Design Is Built Manually---and Inconsistently**

**Copy**

Instructional designers are often expected to build effective learning
programs quickly using disconnected content, inconsistent SME input, and
limited time.

As a result:

-   learning quality varies,

-   measurable outcomes are often unclear,

-   and skill verification becomes difficult to achieve consistently.

**Closing Statement**

Without a structured design process, organizations struggle to
standardize learning effectiveness and workforce capability measurement.

**🟨 SECTION 3 --- WHAT THE MID GENERATOR DOES**

**Headline**

**A Structured Assistant for Measurable Learning Design**

**Intro Copy**

The MID Generator uses measurable instructional design principles to
automatically generate structured learning blueprints aligned to
job-relevant skills and performance expectations.

Instead of starting from scratch, instructional designers begin with a
strong measurable design foundation that can be reviewed, refined, and
customized for organizational needs.

**🧩 PROCESS FLOW**

(Visual workflow highly recommended)

**🔹 Upload Existing Content**

Use existing PowerPoints, SOPs, manuals, job aids, or other learning
materials.

**🔹 Generate a Structured Learning Blueprint**

Automatically create:

-   measurable skill outcomes,

-   aligned evaluations,

-   instructional recommendations,

-   and assessment structures.

**🔹 Refine and Customize**

Instructional designers review, enhance, and adapt the blueprint while
maintaining measurable design integrity.

**🔹 Build Measurable Capability**

Deliver learning designed to build and verify real-world workforce
capability.

**🟦 SECTION 4 --- WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT**

**Headline**

**Designed Around Skills---Not Just Content**

**Copy**

Unlike traditional course development approaches that focus primarily on

This includes:

-   defining expected performance,

-   identifying conditions for success,

-   aligning evaluations to skill outcomes,

-   and supporting verification of learning application on the job.

**Closing Statement**

The result is learning designed to consistently build measurable
capability---not just deliver content.
