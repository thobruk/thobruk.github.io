WHY THE PROGRESSION SECTION
Why Organizations Progress Through the Engagement Levels
Building Practitioners
Organizations begin by learning to design and measure capability initiatives.
As practitioners gain experience, they develop the ability to create measurable learning and collect meaningful capability data.
Demonstrating Value
As organizations gain experience, they need deeper guidance on interpreting data, identifying root causes, and improving results.
Capability intelligence begins to inform learning decisions and future investments.
Scaling the Practice
Organizations pursuing workforce capability visibility require additional support for governance, reporting, leadership engagement, and organizational adoption.
Capability intelligence becomes part of how workforce development decisions are made.
