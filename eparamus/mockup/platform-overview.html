IMPACT Platform

The Platform for Measurable Workforce Capability

IMPACT helps organizations define, build, and verify workforce capability through a connected system for measurable learning, skill verification, and workforce insight.

Purpose-built to help organizations move beyond training activity and gain visibility into what learning is actually producing.

The connected system

One Connected System. Four Core Capabilities.

Most organizations use disconnected tools to design learning, track completion, measure activity, and manage workforce skills. IMPACT connects these functions into one measurable capability system—so learning, performance expectations, and workforce insight stay aligned over time.
