MID Generator

Build Measurable Learning in a Fraction of the Time

The MID Generator helps organizations create structured, measurable learning designs focused on job-relevant skills, aligned evaluations, and real-world performance outcomes.

Reduce design time, improve consistency, and build learning that supports measurable workforce capability.

The challenge

Most Learning Design Is Built Manually—and Inconsistently

Instructional designers are often expected to build effective learning programs quickly using disconnected content, inconsistent SME input, and limited time.

As a result:

  • learning quality varies
  • measurable outcomes are often unclear
  • skill verification becomes difficult to achieve consistently

Without a structured design process, organizations struggle to standardize learning effectiveness and workforce capability measurement.

What it does

A Structured Assistant for Measurable Learning Design

The MID Generator uses measurable instructional design principles to automatically generate structured learning blueprints aligned to job-relevant skills and performance expectations. Instead of starting from scratch, instructional designers begin with a strong measurable design foundation that can be reviewed, refined, and customized for organizational needs.

Automatically create:

  • aligned evaluations
  • instructional recommendations
  • assessment structures
03

Refine and Customize

Instructional designers review, enhance, and adapt the blueprint while maintaining measurable design integrity.

04

Build Measurable Capability

Deliver learning designed to build and verify real-world workforce capability.

What makes it different

Designed Around Skills—Not Just Content

Unlike traditional course development approaches that focus primarily on organizing information, the MID Generator structures learning around measurable job-relevant skills.

This includes:

  • defining expected performance
  • identifying conditions for success
  • aligning evaluations to skill outcomes
  • supporting verification of learning application on the job

The result is learning designed to consistently build measurable capability—not just deliver content.
