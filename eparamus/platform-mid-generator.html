About

MID generator

Turn vague role descriptions into precise, measurable, instruction-ready definitions in minutes.

How it works
1

Input a role or goal

Paste a job description, a business objective, or a performance gap. The generator works from any starting point.

2

Review the generated MID

eParamus produces a structured definition with measurable indicators, context, and scope — ready to review and edit.

3

Approve and publish

Once confirmed, the MID becomes the shared reference for content design, assessment, and capability tracking.

4

Connect to programs

Link the MID to any learning initiative so every program has a defined, verifiable target from day one.
