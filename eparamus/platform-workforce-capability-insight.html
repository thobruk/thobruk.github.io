The problem

Most Learning Data Stops at Activity

Traditional learning systems can track completions, attendance, and participation — but rarely provide visibility into capability development.

Traditional systems track
  • Completions
  • Attendance
  • Survey responses
  • Training participation
But rarely provide visibility into
  • What skills employees developed
  • Whether skills are applied on the job
  • How learning impacts capability over time

Without measurable skill data, organizations struggle to understand workforce capability and readiness.

From learning data to workforce insight

Why it matters

Capability Visibility Changes the Role of Learning

When organizations can define, build, and verify measurable workforce capability, learning becomes more than a training function.

It becomes a source of actionable workforce insight that supports:

  • organizational readiness
  • talent development
  • operational performance
  • strategic decision-making

Workforce capability visibility helps organizations understand not just what learning was delivered—but what capability was built.

Continuous improvement

A Closed-Loop System for Workforce Capability Development

Because measurable learning design, skill verification, and workforce insight are connected within the IMPACT platform, organizations can continuously improve both learning effectiveness and workforce capability development over time.

This creates a measurable feedback loop that helps organizations:

  • strengthen learning programs
  • identify capability gaps
  • support employee growth
  • align workforce development to organizational needs
Practical adoption

Start Small. Build Capability Visibility Over Time.

Organizations don't need enterprise-wide transformation to begin gaining workforce capability insight. Many begin by measuring one learning program and gradually expand measurable capability practices across additional programs, teams, and business units over time.
